Corporate Christmas Flowers for Your Office

Home › Flower Notes › Corporate Christmas Flowers for Your Office

By Lynn15 October 20264 min read

Businesses plan Christmas earlier than individual shoppers. Office managers and small business owners are typically finalising party bookings, supplier lists and gifting budgets in October and November, well before the general public starts thinking about it. If flowers are part of that plan — for reception, a client list, or the office party — this is roughly how it works and when to get it booked. Full details of what we offer are on our corporate flowers page; this is the short, practical version for Christmas specifically.

Weekly & Fortnightly Office Flowers Through December

If reception and meeting rooms already get a regular delivery, December doesn't have to be any different — we simply carry the arrangement through in a festive palette for the run-up to Christmas. If you don't currently have a standing order, it's worth setting one up before the season gets busy: a weekly or fortnightly slot means someone isn't relying on remembering to place a one-off order when things get hectic.

A standing weekly delivery means reception keeps looking presented through December without anyone having to remember to reorder.

One-Off Christmas Party & Event Flowers

For a one-off office Christmas party or client event, we put together table centrepieces, an entrance display, or festive florals to suit the space — whether that's a rented venue or your own offices. These are booked as a single event order rather than a subscription, but they still need proper notice: florals for a party are competing with wreaths and bulk gifting for the same December production time, so 3-4 weeks' lead time is a sensible minimum, longer if the date falls in the second half of December.

Table centrepieces and entrance displays for an office party, arranged to suit the venue.

Client & Staff Gifting

For client and staff Christmas gifts, we handle bulk bouquets from a single list, with branded packaging and a personalised note on each one — so the business behind the gift is clear without every recipient needing to be dealt with individually. This is the one that catches people out on timing: a list of 10 is straightforward on short notice, but anything upwards of 20-30 recipients needs coordinating properly, so treat gifting lists the same as a bulk order, not a last-minute add-on.

Bulk client and staff gifts, arranged from a single list with branded packaging and a personalised note per recipient.

Lead Times, in Short

  • Standing weekly/fortnightly orders — set up any time, but agree your December schedule by early November so a slot's guaranteed.
  • Party & event florals — 3-4 weeks' notice minimum; more for dates in the last two weeks before Christmas.
  • Client & staff gifting (20+ recipients) — confirm your list by early November; December capacity for bulk runs is limited.
