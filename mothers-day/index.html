Mother's Day Flowers FAQ

Questions customers ask

❓

What's the last day I can order for Mother's Day delivery?

We don't set a fixed cut-off in advance, as it depends how busy we are that year — the golden rule is simply to order as early as you can. If you're leaving it late, call 01484 868539 and we'll always do our best to help, though gift baskets and potted plants need a little more notice than a standard bouquet.

❓

Can you deliver flowers to a different address, like my mum's house?

Yes — most Mother's Day orders are delivered straight to Mum rather than collected. Just give us her address and any message for the card, and we'll take care of delivery across Denby Dale, Huddersfield, Barnsley and the surrounding villages.

❓

What's included in a Mother's Day gift basket?

Our gift baskets are hand-arranged with a mix of seasonal blooms — think roses, ranunculus, peonies and lavender — tied with ribbon in a keepsake wicker basket she can use again once the flowers are gone. Let us know her favourite colours and we'll style it around them.

❓

Should I choose a bouquet or a potted plant for Mother's Day?

It comes down to what Mum will enjoy most — a hand-tied bouquet makes an instant impact but lasts a week or two, while a potted plant (hydrangeas are always popular for Mother's Day) keeps going for months with a little care. If you're not sure, give us a call and we'll help you choose.

❓

Do you deliver on Mother's Day itself, or does it need to arrive before?

We can normally arrange delivery in the days leading up to Mother's Day — call us on 01484 868539 to check availability if you specifically need delivery on the Sunday itself.
