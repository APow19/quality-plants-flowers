Funeral Flowers FAQ

Questions families ask

❓

How much do funeral flowers cost in Huddersfield and Barnsley?

Costs depend on size and flowers used, from a simple posy to a full casket spray. Call 01484 868539 and we'll talk through options and give you a clear price before you order.

❓

Do you deliver to Barnsley, Penistone and surrounding villages?

Yes — direct to funeral directors, crematoriums, churches and homes across Barnsley, Penistone, Holmfirth and the rest of HD8. Please call us to confirm delivery in time for the service.

❓

How quickly can you arrange funeral flowers?

Funeral flowers are often needed at short notice. Call us on 01484 868539 as early as possible with the service date and location, and we'll do our best to deliver in time.

❓

Can you match flowers to a colour scheme or favourite flower?

Yes. Many families ask for a particular colour, favourite flower, or a meaningful word spelt out in blooms. Tell us about the person you're remembering and we'll design accordingly.
