Wedding Flowers, Locally

A wedding florist couples across West & South Yorkshire trust

Quality Plants & Flowers is based on Wakefield Road in Denby Dale, but our wedding flowers travel across the whole of HD8 and beyond. Lynn has spent over 20 years designing bouquets, buttonholes and venue florals for couples marrying in Huddersfield town centre, country barns near Barnsley, village churches around Penistone, and everywhere in between — Holmfirth, Kirkburton, Skelmanthorpe, Scissett, Clayton West, Shelley, Shepley, Emley and Kirkheaton included.

Every wedding starts with a free, unhurried consultation. We'll talk through your colours, your venue and your budget, then design bridal flowers that feel entirely yours — whether that's a relaxed, garden-style bouquet or something structured and contemporary.

Wedding Flowers FAQ

Questions brides & grooms ask

❓

How much do wedding flowers cost in Huddersfield?

Costs vary by venue, guest numbers and flower choices, so we don't quote a single fixed price. We offer a free, no-obligation consultation to put together a personalised quote — for weddings in Huddersfield, Barnsley, Denby Dale or anywhere across HD8.

❓

Do you deliver wedding flowers to Barnsley and Penistone?

Yes. We regularly deliver and set up wedding flowers for venues across Barnsley, Penistone, Holmfirth and the wider HD8 area, as well as in Huddersfield and Denby Dale.

❓

How far in advance should I book my wedding florist?

We recommend booking 6–12 months ahead, especially for popular spring and summer dates — though we always do our best to help with shorter-notice requests too.

❓

What's included in a wedding flowers consultation?

We cover your colour palette, venue, budget and the flowers you love, ending with a personalised recommendation for bouquets, buttonholes and venue flowers. No obligation to book.
